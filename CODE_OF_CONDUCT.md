# Code of Conduct

Participants must be respectful, constructive, and safety-minded. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, credential sharing, malicious code, unauthorized-target instructions, attempts to bypass repository security controls, retaliation for good-faith reports, and pressure to approve unsafe material are not acceptable.

Community proposals and reviews must remain focused on educational, authorized, defensive, and source-backed improvement. Do not place personal data, secrets, sensitive report details, or unreviewed allegations in public profiles, issues, proposals, or comments. Maintain a professional review tone, disclose conflicts where relevant, and respect a reviewer’s recusal or request for clarification.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict application participation, or take other proportionate moderation action to protect the community. Sensitive concerns should use the private reporting path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.
