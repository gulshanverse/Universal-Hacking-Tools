# Phase 7 Web Design Directions

## Three possible approaches

### Theme Name: Signal Archive

**Very Brief Intro:** A disciplined technical atlas that treats cybersecurity knowledge as a navigable archive rather than a dramatic command center. It should feel considered, research-led, and calm under scrutiny.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Field Notebook

**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, editorial investigation journal with material cues and layered annotations. It foregrounds learning progression and evidence while keeping execution boundaries visibly conservative.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Quiet Infrastructure

**Very Brief Intro:** A highly utilitarian public reference system using dense typographic hierarchy, compact panels, and neutral operational tones. It emphasizes speed and clarity over display.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Selected approach: Signal Archive

### Design Movement

**Signal Archive** combines the restraint of editorial technical publishing with the structural clarity of a modern field atlas. It avoids cyberpunk shorthand and instead gives relationships, provenance, and safe learning boundaries the visual weight usually reserved for dashboards.

### Core Principles

1. **Evidence before spectacle:** confidence, sources, and safety boundaries remain readable at every scale.
2. **Cartographic navigation:** thin connection rules, indexed cards, and measurable clusters make a complex knowledge base feel explorable.
3. **Calm operational contrast:** deep ink surfaces identify orientation and action; warm paper surfaces support extended reading.
4. **Bounded interaction:** controls make scope and state explicit, especially for local lab activity.

### Color Philosophy

Ink navy provides the stable reference plane for navigation and visual focus, while warm bone paper carries long-form content without the sterility of pure white. Desaturated teal signals connected knowledge, and one copper accent marks decisions, provenance, and calls to action. Verification never relies on color alone: every status has an explicit label.

### Layout Paradigm

Pages behave like an expandable research atlas: a persistent top index establishes orientation, a narrow contextual rail carries filters or metadata, and content moves through staggered editorial bands rather than identical centered cards. Detail pages keep a readable main column with a relationship margin and an accessible list alternative to graph views.

### Signature Elements

1. Contour-line map motifs and small indexed node marks.
2. Copper rule lines separating evidence, safety, and relationship sections.
3. Compact monospaced provenance labels paired with generous editorial display type.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should reveal more structure, not more decoration. Search, filters, relationship expansion, and lab transitions respond immediately with clearly named states. Keyboard actions are direct; transitions are brief and never obscure safety information.

### Animation

Use opacity and transform-only transitions under 220ms for panels, cards, and relationship expansion. Stagger index cards by 40ms on first appearance, with motion disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Search shortcuts and focus movement remain instant.

### Typography System

Use **IBM Plex Serif** for high-level titles and explanatory reading moments, **IBM Plex Sans** for navigation and body text, and **IBM Plex Mono** for identifiers, evidence labels, and safety state. Headings use a distinct weight and tracking rhythm; labels remain compact and consistently uppercase.

### Brand Essence

**A public, evidence-aware cybersecurity knowledge archive for practitioners who need safe paths from concepts to authorized practice.**

Personality adjectives: **measured, rigorous, navigable**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are direct and factual; CTAs describe the next bounded action; microcopy names uncertainty rather than smoothing it over.

> "Trace the relationship, then inspect the evidence."

> "Open a local fixture lab — isolated, disposable, and authorized."

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark pairs the name with an atlas-index line and a small nested-contour mark. The mark is a three-ring signal map with a copper node, used at clear header scale and as the favicon rather than as decorative filler.

### Signature Brand Color

**Archive Copper — `#C7784D`**: a warm, precise accent reserved for provenance, selected states, and intentional actions.
