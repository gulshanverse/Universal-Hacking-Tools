# Privacy Notice

The platform stores the minimum private application state needed for an account: normalized email, password hash, session records, optional learning goals, private progress, bookmarks, plain-text private notes, minimal lab-attempt summaries, achievements, recommendation snapshots, opt-in community profile fields, controlled proposal/review records, private reports, deterministic reputation events, and audit metadata. The platform does not store plaintext passwords, raw session tokens, raw verification/reset tokens, raw Phase 6 local lab evidence, browser fingerprints, precise location, keystrokes, page-by-page telemetry, or third-party analytics.

Cookies are used only for secure authenticated sessions and CSRF protection. The browser does not store long-lived authentication credentials in localStorage. Private data is served only through authenticated, owner-scoped routes. Account deletion revokes sessions and deletes associated private state. Unpublished community drafts are removed; published contribution history may be anonymized where necessary to preserve a reviewed public change record. Public cybersecurity knowledge and public lab definitions remain in the Git-backed repository.

Sessions and temporary verification/reset records expire. Notes, private reports, progress, safe-lab summaries, and unpublished collaboration state are retained until owner or account deletion unless an incident process requires narrowly scoped preservation. Audit/moderation and published-history retention must be minimized and documented by the deployment operator. Backup retention and deletion behavior are **blocked — external prerequisite unavailable**; immutable backups cannot be represented as immediate physical deletion without provider evidence. This document describes engineering intent and does not claim legal certification or regulatory compliance.
